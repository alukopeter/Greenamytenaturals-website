7 Warning Signs Your Catfish Pond Has Poor Water Quality

Published May 2, 2026 | By Emeka Ibe | Fish Health

Water quality is one of the biggest factors affecting catfish survival and growth. Many fish farmers lose money because they fail to notice early danger signs.

1. Fish Gasping at the Surface

When fish constantly come to the surface to breathe, oxygen levels may be too low. This usually happens from overfeeding or dirty water.

2. Bad Water Smell

Healthy pond water should not smell rotten. A strong smell may indicate ammonia buildup or poor waste management.

3. Unusual Water Colour

Very dark, foamy, or excessively green water can indicate imbalance in pond conditions.

4. Slow Fish Growth

Poor water affects feeding response and nutrient absorption, reducing growth rate.

Conclusion

Monitoring water quality daily can prevent massive fish losses and improve productivity. Farmers should regularly check oxygen levels, feeding habits, and pond cleanliness.
