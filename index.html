About AquaFarm Nigeria

Feeding Nigeria,
One Pond at a Time

Founded in 2009, AquaFarm Nigeria has been at the forefront of aquaculture development across the country, working with smallholder farmers, cooperatives, and commercial investors to build profitable fish farming businesses.

Nigeria is the largest consumer of fish in sub-Saharan Africa, yet over 60% of demand is met by imports. We are changing that — empowering local farmers with world-class techniques, quality fingerlings, and ongoing expert support.

  • Sustainability First

    Eco-friendly pond designs that preserve local ecosystems and water tables.

  • Knowledge Transfer

    We don't just build farms — we train farmers for long-term independence.

  • End-to-End Support

    From site selection and pond construction to harvest and market linkage.
